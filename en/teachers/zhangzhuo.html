Biography

Zhuo Zhang was born in April 1978. She earned a bachelor's degree in Information Engineering in 2001 and a master's degree in Communication and Information Systems in 2005, both from Hohai University. In 2011, she began doctoral studies in hydroinformatics at Hohai University. She has taught at the university's College of Internet of Things Engineering since July 2001 and is a lecturer at Hohai University.

Her research focuses on the theory and applications of biologically inspired visual object detection and the intelligent visual Internet of Things. She led two projects funded by the Fundamental Research Funds for the Central Universities and one project each supported by the Jiangsu Key Laboratory of Power Transmission & Distribution Equipment Technology and the Changzhou Key Laboratory of Sensor Network and Environmental Perception. She also contributed to projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, the Changzhou Technology Support Program, and the Research Fund for the Doctoral Program of Higher Education.

She has published several research papers and one paper on teaching. She has more than ten invention patents either granted or applied for, and she advised students who won second prize in a provincial undergraduate electronic design contest in Jiangsu.
